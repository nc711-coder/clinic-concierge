Cancellation & No-Show Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026 · Approved by Maya Okonkwo-Reyes, PT, DPT, OCS, Clinic Director

At Quillmere Physical Therapy & Sports Rehab, every appointment slot is reserved for one patient and one therapist. When a visit is missed without notice, that time cannot usually be offered to another patient who is waiting for care, and the patient who missed the visit loses momentum in their recovery. Research and our own experience show that patients who attend their visits consistently reach their goals faster and with fewer setbacks. This policy explains what we ask of you, what you can expect from us, and how fees are applied. By scheduling an appointment with Quillmere, you agree to the terms below.

Need to reschedule? Use our online form →

1. Purpose of This Policy

The purpose of this policy is to make sure that appointment times are used fairly and efficiently, that patients are treated consistently, and that our therapists can deliver the plan of care that was designed for each patient. This policy applies to all patients of Quillmere, including patients using commercial insurance, Medicare, TRICARE, workers' compensation, auto insurance, and self-pay patients. It applies to all appointment types, including initial evaluations, follow-up visits, telehealth follow-up visits, dry needling sessions, and running gait analysis sessions. It does not apply to the free 15-minute injury screen.

Nothing in this policy is intended to discourage you from seeking care when you need it. If you are ill, injured, or facing an emergency, please contact us — we will work with you.

2. Definitions

For the purposes of this policy, the following definitions apply. An appointment is any scheduled visit with a Quillmere physical therapist or physical therapist assistant, in person or by telehealth. A timely cancellation is a cancellation or reschedule request received by Quillmere at least 24 hours before the scheduled start time of the appointment. A late cancellation is a cancellation or reschedule request received less than 24 hours before the scheduled start time. A no-show is an appointment that the patient does not attend and does not cancel before the scheduled start time. A late arrival is an arrival more than 15 minutes after the scheduled start time. A recurring schedule is a standing series of appointments reserved for a patient at the same day and time each week.

3. How to Cancel or Reschedule

You may cancel or reschedule an appointment in any of the following ways: by calling our front desk at (703) 555-0142 during business hours; by leaving a voicemail at the same number outside business hours (the time stamp on your voicemail will be used to determine whether the cancellation was timely); by using the "Reschedule" button in the patient portal; or by using the reschedule form on our website. Email and text messages to individual staff members are not an approved way to cancel, because they may not be seen in time. Cancellations made through the website chat assistant are recorded with a time stamp and are accepted.

When you cancel, we will do our best to offer you a new time that keeps your plan of care on track. If you cancel more than one visit in a row, your therapist may contact you to discuss whether your plan of care should be adjusted.

4. Timely Cancellations

There is no charge for a timely cancellation. We understand that schedules change, and we appreciate the notice. Patients who give timely notice are never penalized, regardless of how often they need to reschedule, although frequent rescheduling may lead your therapist to revisit your plan of care with you.

5. Late Cancellations and No-Shows

A fee of $40 will be charged for each late cancellation and each no-show. This fee is the same for all appointment types and all payer types. The late cancellation and no-show fee cannot be billed to your insurance company, Medicare, TRICARE, workers' compensation carrier, or auto insurer, because it is not a charge for a medical service. It is your personal responsibility. The fee will be charged to the card on file in the patient portal, or added to your account balance if no card is on file, and is due before your next scheduled visit.

For patients using a prepaid 6-visit package, a no-show or late cancellation does not use up a package visit; the $40 fee is charged separately.

6. Late Arrivals

If you arrive late, your visit will still end at its scheduled time so that the next patient is not delayed. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may not be able to see you, and you may be asked to reschedule. In that case the visit is treated as a late cancellation under Section 5. If you know you will be late, please call us as soon as possible — if the schedule allows, your therapist may still be able to see you for a shortened visit.

7. Waivers and Exceptions

We recognize that some situations are outside of a patient's control. The late cancellation or no-show fee will be waived in the following situations: (a) when Quillmere closes or delays opening because of weather or another emergency; (b) when the patient experiences a documented medical emergency, hospitalization, or a death in the immediate family; (c) when a late cancellation is requested by Quillmere staff, for example because a therapist is unavailable; and (d) when the patient is a workers' compensation patient whose adjuster cancels or changes the authorization for the visit with less than 24 hours' notice.

In addition, each patient receives one courtesy waiver per calendar year. The first late cancellation or no-show in a calendar year will be waived automatically, without any documentation required, and the patient will be reminded of this policy. The courtesy waiver resets on January 1 and does not carry over to the next year. Courtesy waivers apply per patient, not per family or per account.

Requests for a waiver under (b) should be made to our Front Desk & Billing Manager within 14 days of the missed visit. Documentation may be requested. Waiver decisions are made by the Clinic Director and are final.

8. Repeated No-Shows

Patients who have three (3) no-shows within any 90-day period will be removed from their recurring schedule. After removal, the patient may continue care but may book only same-week appointments, based on availability, until the Clinic Director approves a return to a recurring schedule. Late cancellations are not counted toward this limit; only no-shows are counted. Courtesy-waived no-shows still count toward the three-no-show limit, even though no fee is charged.

Before a patient is removed from a recurring schedule, the front desk will make reasonable efforts to contact the patient after the second no-show to discuss any barriers to attending, such as transportation, work schedule changes, or cost concerns.

9. Telehealth Visits

The same rules apply to telehealth follow-up visits. If you are unable to connect to a telehealth visit because of a technical problem on our side, no fee will be charged. If you do not join the video visit within 15 minutes of the start time and have not contacted us, the visit will be recorded as a no-show.

10. Minors

For patients under 18, the parent or legal guardian who scheduled the visit is responsible for cancellations, late arrivals, and fees. A parent or guardian must be present at the first visit and must be on site or reachable by phone for follow-up visits.

11. Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact Tom Albright, Front Desk & Billing Manager, at (703) 555-0142, or ask at the front desk. We are always happy to talk through scheduling options that work for you.
