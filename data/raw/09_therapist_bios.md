# Meet Our Team

## Dr. Maya Okonkwo-Reyes, PT, DPT, OCS — Clinic Director

Maya founded Quillmere in 2017 after eight years in hospital outpatient orthopedics. She is a Board-Certified Orthopaedic Clinical Specialist (OCS) and treats neck pain, low back pain, sciatica, and complex spine cases. She is one of two Quillmere therapists certified in dry needling.

- **Sees patients:** Monday–Friday, daytime
- **Education:** Doctor of Physical Therapy; residency in orthopedic physical therapy
- **Outside the clinic:** gardener, amateur ceramicist, and reluctant 5K runner

## Dr. Daniel Hsu, PT, DPT, SCS

Daniel is a Board-Certified Sports Clinical Specialist (SCS) who works with high school, college and weekend athletes. His specialties are ACL rehab, return-to-sport testing, overuse injuries in runners, and running gait analysis. He has covered local high school football and soccer as a sideline volunteer.

- **Sees patients:** Tuesday and Thursday evenings, and Saturdays
- **Best fit for:** sports injuries, runners, post-ACL reconstruction
- **Outside the clinic:** trail running and coaching youth soccer

## Dr. Priya Natarajan, PT, DPT

Priya focuses on vestibular rehabilitation — dizziness, vertigo, BPPV, and balance disorders — and on post-concussion care for students and adults. She builds graded return-to-learn and return-to-play plans in coordination with schools and physicians.

- **Sees patients:** Monday, Wednesday and Friday
- **Best fit for:** dizziness, vertigo, concussion, balance problems
- **Outside the clinic:** rock climbing and Carnatic music

## Dr. Marcus Bell, PT, DPT

Marcus specializes in rehabilitation after surgery — total knee and hip replacements, rotator cuff repairs, and fracture recovery — and in fall-prevention programs for older adults. He is certified in dry needling.

- **Sees patients:** Monday–Thursday
- **Best fit for:** post-surgical rehab, joint replacement, balance and fall prevention in older adults
- **Outside the clinic:** woodworking and grilling

## Elena Vasquez, PTA

Elena is a licensed Physical Therapist Assistant. She treats patients at follow-up visits under a physical therapist's plan of care and is known for making home exercise programs simple and memorable. Elena does not perform initial evaluations or dry needling.

- **Sees patients:** Monday–Saturday
