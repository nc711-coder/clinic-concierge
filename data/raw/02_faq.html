Getting Started

Do I need a referral or prescription from my doctor?

For most commercial insurance plans, no — you can come straight to us. Medicare and TRICARE patients need a physician to sign the plan of care; we send it to your doctor for you after your evaluation. Some HMO plans also require a referral from your primary care provider, so check with your plan.

Can I see a physical therapist without a referral?

Yes, in most cases. See "Do I need a referral or prescription from my doctor?" above for the exceptions (Medicare, TRICARE and some HMO plans).

How do I book my first appointment?

Call (703) 555-0142, fill out the booking form on our website, or use the chat assistant in the corner of this page. New patients book a 60-minute initial evaluation.

How soon can I be seen?

Most new patients are seen within 5 business days. Call us if your need is urgent and we'll check for cancellations.

What happens at my first visit?

Your first visit is a 60-minute evaluation with a Doctor of Physical Therapy. We'll talk about your history and goals, test your strength, movement and balance, explain what we find, and start treatment. You'll leave with a plan and a few exercises.

How long is the first appointment?

About 60 minutes. Please arrive 15 minutes early to check in.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, your referral or prescription if you have one, and a list of your current medications. Wear comfortable clothes that let us see and move the area being treated — shorts for knee problems, a tank top for shoulder problems.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing and sneakers. Shorts are helpful for hip, knee and ankle problems; a tank top or loose t-shirt for shoulder and neck problems.

Do I have to fill out paperwork?

Yes. You'll get a link to our patient portal when you book. Please complete your intake forms at least 24 hours before your first visit so we can review them ahead of time.

Can I do my first visit by video?

No. Initial evaluations are always in person because we need to examine you hands-on. Some follow-up visits can be done by telehealth.

Can I come in on Saturday for my evaluation?

Saturday mornings (8 AM – 12 PM) are reserved for follow-up visits and sports rehab. Initial evaluations are scheduled Monday through Friday.

Visits & Treatment

How many visits will I need?

It depends on your condition and goals. Many patients come 1–2 times a week for 4–8 weeks. Your therapist will give you an estimate after your evaluation and update it as you progress.

How long is a follow-up visit?

Follow-up visits are 45–60 minutes. Telehealth follow-ups are about 30 minutes.

Will I see the same therapist every time?

We try to keep you with the same physical therapist. Some follow-up visits may be with Elena Vasquez, our Physical Therapist Assistant, working under your PT's plan of care.

Who should I see for a sports injury?

Dr. Daniel Hsu is our Board-Certified Sports Clinical Specialist. He sees patients Tuesday and Thursday evenings and Saturdays.

Do you treat dizziness or vertigo?

Yes. Dr. Priya Natarajan specializes in vestibular rehabilitation for dizziness, vertigo (including BPPV) and balance problems, and in concussion recovery.

Do you treat concussions?

Yes — see Dr. Priya Natarajan. She creates step-by-step return-to-learn and return-to-play plans.

Do you offer dry needling?

Yes. Dry needling is offered by Dr. Maya Okonkwo-Reyes and Dr. Marcus Bell. It can be added to a regular visit or booked as a standalone 30-minute session.

Does dry needling hurt?

Most people feel a brief twitch or ache in the muscle. Soreness for a day afterward is common. Your therapist will explain what to expect and whether it's a good fit for you.

What is a gait analysis?

A 75-minute running assessment with treadmill video, a strength and mobility screen, and a written report. It's self-pay only. Ask for Dr. Daniel Hsu.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes — our free 15-minute injury screen, by phone or in person. It's not a full evaluation and we can't diagnose during a screen, but it helps you decide whether physical therapy makes sense.

Insurance & Billing

What insurance do you take?

We're in-network with Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield, CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare Part B and TRICARE East. We also accept workers' compensation and auto injury (PIP/MedPay) claims with a claim number.

Do you take Medicaid?

We are not in-network with Virginia Medicaid / Cardinal Care. You're welcome to pay our self-pay rates.

Do you accept Kaiser?

No, Kaiser Permanente is out-of-network. Self-pay rates are available and we can give you a superbill to submit yourself.

How much does a visit cost without insurance?

Our self-pay rates are $165 for an initial evaluation and $120 for a follow-up visit. Pay in full at your visit and get 10% off. See our fee schedule for all services.

What are your self-pay prices?

Initial evaluation $165 · Follow-up $120 · Telehealth follow-up $95 · Dry needling add-on $45 · Standalone dry needling $85 · Gait analysis $175 · 6-visit package $630. A 10% prompt-pay discount applies to individual self-pay visits paid in full at the time of service (not packages or gait analysis).

Do you have package pricing?

Yes. Six prepaid follow-up visits cost $630 and are valid for 6 months. The prompt-pay discount does not apply to packages.

Can I use my HSA or FSA card?

Yes. We accept HSA and FSA cards along with credit and debit cards. We do not accept personal checks.

Do you take checks?

No, we don't accept personal checks. Credit, debit, HSA and FSA cards are accepted.

When is my copay due?

Copays and self-pay amounts are due at the time of service.

Will you check my insurance benefits for me?

Yes. Our front desk verifies your benefits before your first visit. Coverage is ultimately determined by your plan, and you're responsible for knowing your plan's visit limits.

Scheduling & Policies

What is your cancellation policy?

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged a $40 fee, which is not billed to insurance. See our full Cancellation & No-Show Policy for details.

What if I'm running late?

Call us. If you're more than 15 minutes late we may need to reschedule, and the visit is treated as a late cancellation.

What are your hours?

Monday–Thursday 7 AM – 7 PM, Friday 7 AM – 5 PM, Saturday 8 AM – 12 PM. Closed Sunday. Holiday hours are posted separately.

Where are you located and where do I park?

418 Cedar Mill Road, Suite 200, Ashford Falls, VA 20199. Parking is free in the lot behind the building. The accessible entrance and elevator are on the Cedar Mill Road side.

Is your building wheelchair accessible?

Yes. Use the Cedar Mill Road entrance, where there's a ramp and an elevator to Suite 200.

Records & Privacy

How do I get a copy of my records?

Submit a request through the patient portal or contact our Privacy Officer, Janelle Ortiz, at privacy@quillmere-pt.example. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days, and your first electronic copy is free.

Can the chat assistant see my medical records?

No. The website chat assistant answers general questions and helps with booking. It cannot access your medical records. For questions about your own care, call us or use the patient portal.
