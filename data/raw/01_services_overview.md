# Our Services

Quillmere Physical Therapy & Sports Rehab helps people in Ashford Falls and the surrounding area move without pain, recover from surgery, and get back to the sports and activities they love. Every plan of care starts with a one-on-one evaluation with a licensed Doctor of Physical Therapy.

## Orthopedic Physical Therapy

Hands-on treatment and exercise for back pain, neck pain, and injuries to the shoulder, knee, hip and ankle. Typical plans combine manual therapy, strengthening, mobility work and a home exercise program you can actually stick to.

**Led by:** Dr. Maya Okonkwo-Reyes (spine and orthopedics)

## Sports Injury Rehabilitation

From sprained ankles to tendinitis to ACL tears, we build rehab around your sport and your season. Our return-to-sport testing gives you and your coach objective numbers before you go back to full play.

**Led by:** Dr. Daniel Hsu, Board-Certified Sports Clinical Specialist. Evening and Saturday appointments available.

## Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

We work with your surgeon's protocol after ACL reconstruction, rotator cuff repair, and total knee or hip replacement. Most patients start therapy within one to two weeks of surgery.

**Led by:** Dr. Marcus Bell

## Vestibular & Concussion Rehabilitation

Treatment for dizziness, vertigo (including BPPV), balance problems and post-concussion symptoms, with gradual return-to-activity plans for students and athletes.

**Led by:** Dr. Priya Natarajan

## Dry Needling

Dry needling uses thin filament needles to target tight, painful muscle tissue. It is usually added to a regular visit, but standalone sessions are available. Dry needling at Quillmere is performed only by Dr. Okonkwo-Reyes and Dr. Bell, who hold advanced certification.

## Running Gait Analysis

A 75-minute session on the treadmill with slow-motion video, a strength and mobility screen, and a written report with drills and shoe recommendations. Gait analysis is **self-pay only** — it is not billed to insurance.

## Free 15-Minute Injury Screen

Not sure if physical therapy is right for you? Talk with a therapist for 15 minutes by phone or in person, at no cost. A screen is not a full evaluation and does not include a diagnosis — it helps you decide on next steps.

## Telehealth Follow-Ups

Established patients can do some follow-up visits by secure video. Initial evaluations are always in person.
