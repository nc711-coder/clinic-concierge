## Insurance
- In-network: Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield, CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare Part B, TRICARE East
- Accepted with claim info: Workers' compensation (claim number + adjuster contact required); auto injury (PIP/MedPay)
- Out-of-network: Virginia Medicaid / Cardinal Care, Kaiser Permanente, Humana (commercial). Patients may pay self-pay rates; Quillmere provides a superbill on request.
- Referrals: most commercial plans do not require a physician referral to start. Medicare and TRICARE patients need a physician to sign the plan of care; Quillmere coordinates this.

## Booking rules
- New patients book an initial evaluation (60 min). Initial evaluations are not offered on Saturdays or by telehealth.
- Complete intake forms in the patient portal at least 24 hours before the first visit.
- Arrive 15 minutes early for the first visit. Bring photo ID, insurance card, referral or prescription if you have one, and a medication list. Wear comfortable clothing.
- Ways to book: phone, website booking form, or the website chat assistant.
- Typical wait for a new-patient evaluation: within 5 business days.

## Cancellation & no-show policy
- Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance.
- Late cancellation (<24 hours) or no-show fee: $40. Not billed to insurance.
- One courtesy waiver per patient per calendar year (buried in section 7 of the long policy page).
- Fee also waived for clinic weather closures and documented emergencies.
- 3 no-shows within 90 days: patient is removed from their recurring appointment schedule and may book only same-week appointments.
- Patients more than 15 minutes late may be asked to reschedule (and the visit counts as a late cancellation).

## Financial responsibility (from the scanned agreement)
- Copays and self-pay amounts are due at time of service.
- Balances unpaid after 90 days may be sent to a collection agency, after two statements and one phone call.
- Patients are responsible for knowing their plan's visit limits.
- Statement balances under $5 are written off.
